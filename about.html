Meet the Founder

From Italy to Norway

Hei, I am Serena.

My journey began in the sun-drenched piazzas of Italy, where I spent years living, working, and soaking in the dolce vita. I learned that Italy isn't just a destination; it's a feeling. It's the smell of fresh basil, the sound of laughter over a shared meal, and the slow rhythm of the afternoon.

When I moved to Norway, I fell in love with the nature and the people, but I realized something was missing. Many travelers were visiting Italy, but few were seeing the real Italy.

I founded Ekte Italia to be that bridge. I want to show Norwegians the hidden gems, the authentic homes, and the local friends I left behind. I don't just book you a house; I welcome you into a community.

Serena
